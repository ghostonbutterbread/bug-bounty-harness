# XSS consumer reachability

Load from `xss` when a plausible sink or renderer is known but its claimed
consumer does not mount, or a vector is about to be parked as gated. This is a
**stop classifier**, not a payload menu or permission to cross scope, ownership,
rate, CAPTCHA, or human-visible impact boundaries. The selected XSS lane owns
browser proof, cleanup, and path-scoped closure.

## Classify what actually stopped

| Observation | Working class | Next question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The value reaches the claimed consumer but changes at validation, rendering, or execution | Defense | Trace the first observed boundary with `references/defense-chain.md`; a filter response alone does not prove the consumer mounted. |
| Source or bundle names a sink, but the claimed view is hidden, empty, redirected, flag-off, or not configured | Reachability | Which owned route, writer, role, or ordinary setup could mount it? A static sink is not a proven source-to-sink path. |
| The consumer is outside published scope, staff testing is prohibited, or the proposed artifact would have disallowed human-visible or public effects | Policy | Stop that action; retain a scoped alternative or a precise scope-decision wake condition. A permitted, inert, correlated staff-facing blind canary is governed by `blind-xss`, not automatically excluded here. |
| Another route appears to use the same downstream helper | Distinct ingress question | Reuse the helper analysis, but check the route's writer, validation, filter, storage, and decode stages before transferring a negative. A changed parser or sink is a separate consumer branch. |

Distinguish an HTML route's client-side 404 from a writer/configuration API
response. Neither status by itself establishes server enforcement or absence of
a consumer; verify the relevant owned effect or denial under `assumption-testing`.

## Reachability: choose an evidenced legal mount

The list is a decision aid, not a mandatory sequence. Skip disproven paths and
stay within published rules and owned, rate-safe fixtures.

- **Normal self-service setup:** product settings, integrations, plugins, project
  flags, or permitted free enrollment. Load `account-testing-policy` for an
  owned account, role, object, or configuration change. A paid plan or an
  internal-only cohort is not a default test fixture.
- **Writer API behind a UI claim:** recovered client code may name a write
  operation while navigation is hidden. Load `assumption-testing`; establish an
  owned baseline, call the exact in-scope operation only when authorized, and
  verify its server-side write or denial. A response code or client flag alone
  is inconclusive. Do not patch a vendor's client flags to impersonate staff or
  an internal cohort as a substitute for normal customer enablement.
- **Other owned session or producer:** an authorized role, import, SDK, or form
  may reach the same sink. Keep its ingress and transforms distinct; use
  `references/source-acquisition.md` to find writers and `xss-lifecycle` for
  later consumers. A reachable sibling path may merit its own context-matched
  probes even when the original view remains absent.
- **Human challenge:** follow `captcha-policy` for an authorized browser flow;
  do not bypass a challenge to sustain unrelated testing. CAPTCHA bypass as
  the security question itself belongs to that policy and the program rules.

If feasible ordinary authorized remedies fail and the remaining prerequisite
is external, use `blockers` with remediation evidence and a wake condition.
Otherwise keep the exact missing source-to-consumer link open rather than call
the path `exhausted` or declare a sanitizer negative. A confirmed impossible
link may be closed only for the tested source, route, and consumer; do not
transfer that conclusion to sibling routes or parsers.

## Once mounted, return to the XSS lane

For a value that reaches a consumer, locate the first changed representation
with `references/defense-chain.md`. Use `xss-payload-engineering` and its
mechanism-ideation or idea-tree references for materially different parser
mechanisms, not to avoid resolving a missing mount. A safe render through one
helper does not settle another renderer. Preserve the producer, consumer,
transform, owned fixture, observed boundary, and next discriminator through
`attempt-recording-policy`; the selected lane decides proof and disposition.
