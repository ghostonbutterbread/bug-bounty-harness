## Intent

Stop probe-stage automatic redirect following and persistence of sensitive redirect destinations and reflected redirect titles. Retain normal authorized subdomain probing, normal 200 title extraction and no-response HTTPS-to-HTTP fallback. Prevent blind following during takeover rechecks and surface deferred redirect coverage; preserve passive providers and local modes.

## Implemented contract

3xx responses are not followed or read; status is retained, title empty, content length zero, and a Location-bearing response records `[redacted]` rather than the Location or final URL. A response hook captures headers per request before HTTPX can parse malformed Location, with no shared hook mutation and request-local state removed after send. The helper rejects a client without the capture hook first before every request; callers must not mutate its hooks in flight. A response of any status prevents scheme fallback even if subsequent parsing or body decoding fails. Failures before headers permit HTTP fallback; errors are not printed. Normal non-3xx title and body length extraction remain. Takeover rechecks explicitly disable following and report observed redirects and recheck errors. Provider hostnames are canonicalized and validated before suffix comparison, mutation and dispatch; exclusions and available allow scope are checked on probe URLs, including fallback. Coverage records checked, skipped, deferred and failed counts and distinct statuses. URLScan task URLs explicitly validate their authority before IDNA canonicalization and accept case-insensitive HTTP(S), Unicode and valid numeric ports. Progress separates probe outcomes and completed takeover attempts from checked/deferred/failed results; `total_probed` excludes skipped stubs but includes no-response attempts. Legacy scan records and dead counts retain skipped stubs; see `docs/subdomain-probe-redirects.md` for compatibility and limitations. This is not DNS, proxy, connection, or general network-egress containment.
