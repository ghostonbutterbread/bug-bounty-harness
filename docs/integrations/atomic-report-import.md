## Implemented contract

`sync_reports` and `ManualHunter.ingest` use Core's locked `import_finding(..., packet_mode='evidence_only')` instead of a separate reservation and materialization. Manual completed duplicates retain the nonzero return, output and optional comment behavior without adding a sighting. Sync completed duplicates remain skipped; only Core's exact `finding FID already assigned: ` ValueError prefix is classified as an FID conflict in sync. Other ValueErrors and I/O errors propagate from the API/CLI.

The watcher catches and reports an individual ingestion exception, **does not advance that file's handled fingerprint**, and retries on the next poll, including after restart. A returned duplicate is still handled; unchanged successfully handled files are skipped. Every failed poll still sleeps for `max(0.2, poll_interval)`; persistent errors are logged and retried indefinitely rather than in a hot loop. Keep the original candidate identity and packet mode for Core to recover a pending import.
